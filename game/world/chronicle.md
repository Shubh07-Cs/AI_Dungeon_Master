## Prologue — A World Between Decay and Light

There was a time before the Merge — before sorcery bled into silicon, before the ley lines were rerouted through fiber-optic cables threaded through the bones of dead gods. The elders speak of it in half-remembered liturgies, their voices crackling like corrupted audio files. But that world is gone. Overwritten.

What remains is **Tenebris Machina** — a world stitched together from the carcass of a fallen age of magic and the cold skeleton of a technological ascension that never quite completed. Gothic cathedrals pierce a sky permanently bruised with chemical twilight, their spires crowned not with crosses but with satellite arrays that still search for signals from gods who stopped transmitting centuries ago. Gargoyles perch on data-relay towers, their stone eyes replaced with camera lenses that blink red in the acid rain. In the undercities, bioluminescent fungi grow alongside server racks humming with the trapped echoes of ancient incantations.

The people endure. They always do. Merchants hawk rune-etched circuit boards in markets lit by holographic torches. Street surgeons splice cybernetic implants powered by captured spell fragments into the arms of soldiers and thieves alike. Children play in alleyways where the cobblestones glow faintly with residual enchantment, their laughter drowned out by the distant throb of machines that no one remembers building.

And beneath it all — beneath the stone, beneath the wires, beneath the layers of civilization piled like scar tissue over an old wound — the **Chrono-Engine** turns. Ancient. Patient. Counting every death, every choice, every divergent timeline like a god with an abacus. It does not judge. It does not intervene. It simply *records*. And when reality fractures — when a thread is cut too soon, when a story demands another draft — it *rewinds*.

No one knows who built it. No one knows how to stop it.

Some say it is mercy.  
Some say it is a prison.  
Aelias is about to find out which.

## Chapter 1 — The Descent

### Entry 1 · Turn 0 · *Neon Crypts — Sector 7 Entrance*

The rain hasn't stopped in three days. It comes down in sheets of grey-green chemical mist, beading on every surface, hissing faintly where it touches exposed circuitry. The Neon Crypts rise from the urban decay like a wound — a cathedral-fortress from the old world, its flying buttresses fused with corroded steel girders, its stained-glass windows long shattered and replaced with flickering holo-panels that display warnings in a language no one alive can read.

Aelias stands at the Sector 7 entrance. The massive door — twenty feet of iron and rune-scored titanium — is ajar. Not forced. *Invited*. The Corroded Sector Key in their pack hums faintly, its circuitry pulsing in sympathetic resonance with something deep below.

The air here tastes of ozone and old incense. From beyond the threshold, a sound: the rhythmic drip of water onto stone, underlaid by the barely perceptible whine of ancient machinery still grinding through its purpose. Neon light spills from within — sickly violet and arterial red — painting the rain-slicked ground in the colors of a fresh bruise.

There are stories about Sector 7. The scavengers who mapped the outer crypts call it the **Threshold of the Unnamed** — the point past which reliable intel ends and rumor begins. Glitch-Wraiths patrol the corridors, they say. Constructs of corrupted data given spectral form, drawn to the warmth of living thought like moths to a dying star. And deeper still, in chambers that predate the Merge itself, things older than silicon or sorcery stir in their patient sleep.

Aelias adjusts the Runic Vibro-Dagger at their hip. The blade emits a faint harmonic — a sound more felt than heard, like a tuning fork pressed against bone. The Synth-Leather Jerkin creaks as they roll their shoulders. Two Minor Elixirs of Restoration clink softly in the pack. Not enough. Never enough.

But the door is open. The Engine is watching. And the only way out of this story is through.

*The descent begins.*
